Zašto sve ovo.Why all this.

Digitalizacija Srbije je jedan od najvažnijih koraka ka prosperitetu i borbi protiv korupcije.

Svaki lični odnos između vlasti i čoveka, kada nema jasnih pravila i procesa, postaje potencijalna koruptivna šema.

Preterana birokratija rađa „svoje ljude” koji su za novac spremni da reše sve probleme. To na kraju vodi kršenju zakona i truljenju sistema.

U ovom dokumentu sakupio sam 60 ideja koje mogu da pomognu da Srbija postane jaka i prosperitetna zemlja, udobna i za njene građane i za strance koji je izaberu kao mesto za život i posao.

Svaka ideja ima isti oblik: u čemu je problem i šta predlažem. Ideje su podeljene u tri nivoa, po tome koliko ih je teško sprovesti.

Digitalising Serbia is one of the most important steps towards prosperity and the fight against corruption.

Any personal relationship between the authorities and a citizen, where there are no clear rules and processes, becomes a potential corruption scheme.

Excessive bureaucracy breeds “people who know people”, ready to solve any problem for money. In the end this leads to broken laws and a rotting system.

In this document I have gathered 60 ideas that can help Serbia become a strong, prosperous country, convenient both for its citizens and for the foreigners who choose it as a place to live and do business.

Each idea has the same shape: what the problem is and what I propose. The ideas are split into three tiers by how hard they are to implement.

Dva nivoa eUprave umesto tri

Problem

Već postoji: eID ima tri načina prijave: lozinka, aplikacija ConsentID i kvalifikovani sertifikat (lična karta sa čipom ili sertifikat u oblaku). Problem: za viši nivo aktivacioni kod se uzima na šalteru ili preko sertifikata, a tri nivoa zbunjuju ljude.

Predlog

Samo dva nivoa. Osnovni: e-pošta i snimak dokumenta. Puni: potvrda identiteta preko banke, lične karte sa čipom ili pošte, po standardu eIDAS, bez šaltera. Stranci dobijaju osnovni nivo odmah, a puni sa boravkom.

eID portal: prijava lozinkom, aplikacijom ConsentID ili sertifikatom

Two eUprava levels instead of three

The problem

Already exists: eID has three ways to log in: a password, the ConsentID app and a qualified certificate (chip ID card or cloud certificate). Problem: for the higher level the activation code is collected at a counter or via a certificate, and three levels confuse people.

The proposal

Just two levels. Basic: email and a document scan. Full: identity confirmed by a bank, a chip ID card or the pošta, under the eIDAS standard, with no counter visit. Foreigners get basic access at once and full access with residence.

The proposal

Standard two-factor authentication (SMS or a TOTP app) and Passkey, as with Google and Apple, plus biometrics in the eUprava app. ConsentID can stay only for qualified signatures.

Problem

The proposal

One chat in the eUprava app. You report a city problem with a photo and location, ask about any state service, or get redirected to the right office. Answers from the responsible offices arrive in the same chat, in plain human language, without bureaucracy. Every report has a status and a deadline, and a public map shows what has been fixed. No more unnecessary phone calls, while the phone line stays for those who need it, in Serbian and English.

№ 47Novi zakonA new law

Lična karta i boravak u Apple i Google Wallet-u

Problem

Fizička kartica se gubi, a za svaku proveru mora da se nosi.

Predlog

Digitalna lična karta i boravišna dozvola u telefonu, po standardu ISO 18013-5 i evropskom EUDI novčaniku, sa istom pravnom snagom kao kartica.

Prednosti

Dokument je uvek u telefonu i ne može se izgubiti kao kartica. Provera traje sekundu (QR ili NFC), a pokazuješ samo ono što je potrebno, na primer samo da si punoletan.

Složenost

Visoka. Potreban je zakon koji digitalnoj ispravi daje istu snagu kao kartici, usklađivanje sa ISO 18013-5 i dogovor sa Apple-om i Google-om. EU uvodi EUDI novčanik, pa Srbija može da prati isti standard.

ID and residence card in Apple and Google Wallet

The problem

Physical cards get lost and must be carried for every check.

The proposal

A digital ID and residence card on the phone, following ISO 18013-5 and the EU EUDI wallet, with the same legal force as the card.

Benefits

The document is always on your phone and cannot be lost like a card. A check takes a second (QR or NFC), and you share only what is needed, for example just that you are over 18.

Difficulty

High. It needs a law giving the digital document the same legal force as the card, ISO 18013-5 compliance and agreements with Apple and Google. The EU is introducing the EUDI wallet, so Serbia can follow the same standard.

Problem

The proposal

The eUprava app gives you a personal QR code for each case. The clerk scans it and the system instantly opens your specific case: what you filed, what is missing and what comes next. You do not have to explain anything, and the clerk only asks specific follow-up questions. The same principle works in every state office, from MUP and APR to the pošta.

One counter serves a live queue for urgent cases and for people who do not want or cannot use online services. Nobody is left out, and the service becomes faster and more personal.

eUprava: provera autentičnosti dokumenata

QR verification on every document

The problem

eUprava already has a document authenticity check: scan the QR code or enter the data by hand, no login needed, and you get the authentic document. But it covers only some documents, so diplomas, contracts and certificates are still checked with phone calls and stamps.

The proposal

Extend the existing service to every new document issued by any state body: ID documents, diplomas, decisions, court rulings, certificates, notarial deeds. One QR format on every document opens a gov.rs page with the data and status: valid, revoked or expired. The page is in Serbian and English, and banks and employers get an open API, so a document is checked in two seconds, with no calls and no extra certificates.

Ne tražiti papire koje država već ima

Problem

Već postoji: od 2016. ZUP (čl. 9 i 103) obavezuje službe da same pribave podatke iz službenih evidencija, a eZUP povezuje 18 baza osam institucija: matične knjige, MUP, Poreska, PIO, NSZ, CROSO, RGZ i pravosuđe. Legalno ubrzanje dokumenata

Problem

Ko hitno treba dokument nema legalan način da ga dobije brže, pa traži „vezu”.

Predlog

Tri zvanične brzine: standard 30 dana, 15 dana uz +50% takse i 7 dana uz dvostruku taksu. Novac ide u budžet službe, ne u nečiji džep. Tako već radi, na primer, Gruzija: pasoš za 10 radnih dana košta 150 lari, za 24 sata 300, a isti dan 350. Velika Britanija nudi pasoš za nedelju dana (fast track) ili za jedan dan (premium) uz višu taksu.

SDA (Gruzija): rokovi i takse za pasoš · GOV.UK: hitno izdavanje pasoša (fast track i premium)

Legal fast track for documents

The problem

Anyone who needs a document urgently has no legal way to get it faster, so they look for “connections”.

The proposal

Three official speeds: standard 30 days, 15 days for +50% and 7 days for double the fee. The money goes to the office budget, not into someone's pocket. This already works in Georgia, for example: a passport in 10 working days costs 150 lari, in 24 hours 300, same day 350. The UK offers a passport in one week (fast track) or one day (premium) for a higher fee.

Međugradske karte onlajn

Problem

Već postoji: Srbijavoz prodaje karte onlajn i u aplikaciji, a Polazak.rs ima red vožnje i onlajn karte za međunarodne autobuske linije. Problem: za većinu domaćih autobusa karta se kupuje na šalteru stanice, a onlajn kartu često treba odštampati.

Predlog

Jedan sistem prodaje karata za sve autobuske prevoznike i stanice: red vožnje, kupovina karticom i karta u telefonu, bez štampanja. Stanične usluge (peronska karta) uključene u cenu.

Polazak.rs: česta pitanja (onlajn karte za međunarodne linije)

Intercity tickets online

The problem

Already exists: Srbijavoz sells tickets online and in its app, and Polazak.rs has timetables and online tickets for international bus lines. Problem: for most domestic buses you still buy the ticket at the station counter, and an online ticket often has to be printed.

The proposal

One ticketing system for all bus carriers and stations: timetable, card payment and a ticket on your phone, no printing. Station charges (peronska karta) included in the price.

№ 20OdmahRight away

Parking jednim QR kodom

Problem

Već postoji: u Beogradu aplikacija Parking servis sa onlajn plaćanjem. Problem: SMS parking radi samo sa srpskim brojem, aplikaciju treba instalirati i registrovati, a svaki grad ima svoja pravila i aplikacije, pa turisti i stranci teško plaćaju.

Predlog

Na svakom parking mestu ili zoni stoji QR kod. Skeniraš ga ili otvoriš jedan sajt, na primer parkovka.gov.rs, uneseš broj zone i registarski broj vozila, izabereš vreme i platiš preko Apple Pay ili Google Pay. Ako ostaviš broj telefona, besplatan SMS te podseti kada parking ističe, a možeš izabrati i e-poštu, što je još jeftinije.

Biznis.rs (jul 2024): manjak parkinga i aparata za kartice · N1 (maj 2021): aplikacije za plaćanje parkinga u Beogradu

Parking with one QR code

The problem

Already exists: in Belgrade the Parking servis app with online payment. Problem: SMS parking works only with a Serbian number, the app has to be installed and registered, and every city has its own rules and apps, so tourists and foreigners struggle to pay.

The proposal

Every parking spot or zone has a QR code. You scan it or open one website, for example parkovka.gov.rs, enter the zone number and your licence plate, choose the time and pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay. Leave a phone number and a free SMS reminds you before parking expires, or choose email, which is even cheaper.

№ 59Novi zakonA new law

Bez nalepnice na vozilu: provera po tablicama

Problem

Posle registracije i obaveznog osiguranja od autoodgovornosti moraš po nalepnicu i papire, iako država već ima sve podatke. Vozač ih nosi samo da bi ih pokazao na kontroli.

Predlog

Izmeniti zakon i ukinuti obavezu nalepnice. Podaci o registraciji i osiguranju su u eUpravi, a saobraćajna policija ima aplikaciju eUprava za zaposlene: po broju tablice odmah vidi da li je osiguranje plaćeno. Baza je učitana i radi i bez interneta, a kada ima veze, proverava najnovije stanje, na primer da osiguranje ističe danas. Velika Britanija je papirni disk ukinula 2014. i proverava vozila kamerama i bazom osiguranja, a Irska ukida svoj disk zakonom.

GOV.UK: ukidanje papirnog poreskog diska (2014) · MIB (UK): policija i kamere proveravaju osiguranje u bazi MID · Gov.ie: zakon o ukidanju papirnog diska (2024)

No sticker on the car: checks by licence plate

The problem

After registration and compulsory autoodgovornost (motor liability) insurance you still have to collect a sticker and papers, although the state already has all the data. Drivers carry them only to show at a check.

The proposal

Change the law and drop the sticker requirement. Registration and insurance data live in eUprava, and traffic police get an eUprava staff app: by licence plate they instantly see whether insurance is paid. The database is preloaded and works offline, and with a connection it checks the latest status, for example that insurance expires today. The UK abolished its paper disc in 2014 and checks vehicles with cameras and an insurance database; Ireland is abolishing its disc by law.

Reforma pošte

1/4

Sve poštanske usluge u jednoj aplikaciji

Problem

Mnogo toga na pošti se i dalje radi na papiru i ručno: obaveštenja, formulari, plaćanja, a red u poslovnici traje.

Predlog

Potpuno digitalizovati sve što je ostalo na papiru. Normalna mobilna aplikacija Pošte Srbije u kojoj svaki korak, od praćenja pošiljke do plaćanja, ide jednostavno i u istom dizajnu kao ostale državne usluge. Poslovnice dobijaju skenere i digitalne procese, pa se usluga obavi za nekoliko minuta.

2/4

Paket jednim QR kodom

Problem

Danas doneseš papirni izveštaj o pošiljci, službenica ručno unosi podatke u sistem, pa uzima ličnu kartu i prepisuje je. Za svaku uslugu na pošti sve iz početka.

Predlog

U aplikaciji Pošte Srbije svaki paket ima QR kod za preuzimanje. Prijava u aplikaciju ide preko eUprave sa dvofaktorskom potvrdom, pa je QR kod unapred potvrđen. Službenica ga skenira i odmah vidi koji je paket, gde se nalazi i kako se preuzima: ništa ne kuca ručno. Ako treba dodatna potvrda, na telefon stiže četvorocifreni kod. Isti QR radi i za plaćanja i druge državne usluge na pošti. Lična karta samo kada treba platiti carinu ili taksu, i to kao poseban korak.

3/4

Carina, deklaracija i plaćanje onlajn

Problem

Ne zna se kako se računaju carina i PDV, deklaracija se popunjava na šalteru (od 23. februara 2026. Pošta je i naplaćuje), a poštar na adresi traži gotovinu.

Predlog

Deklaraciju popunjavaš u aplikaciji: odakle stiže pošiljka i šta je u njoj. Javni kalkulator pokazuje kako se računaju carina, PDV i naknade, i sve se plaća onlajn unapred. Ako ipak treba doplatiti na adresi, poštaru se u aplikaciji odmah pravi NBS IPS QR kod, a primalac plaća na licu mesta iz bankarske aplikacije.

4/4

Carinjenje za jedan radni dan

Problem

Paketi stoje na carini i više od 35 dana, a zakonskog roka za carinjenje nema. Sve se radi ručno, pa je carina preopterećena.

Predlog

Cilj: 99,9% pošiljki ocarinjeno u roku od jednog radnog dana od dolaska. To je moguće bez velikih troškova, jer su deklaracija i plaćanje već onlajn i unapred. Sistem je gotovo potpuno automatizovan, a na odluku automata možeš da se žališ i tada je pregleda živ čovek. Složeni slučajevi se rešavaju onlajn, a po potrebi možeš doći i doneti dokumente na licu mesta. Manje posla za carinu, brže i lakše za sve.

Pošta reform

1/4

All postal services in one app

The problem

Much at the pošta is still done on paper and by hand: notices, forms, payments, and the queue at the branch drags on.

The proposal

Fully digitalise everything still on paper. A proper Pošta Srbije mobile app where every step, from tracking to payment, is simple and in the same design as other state services. Branches get scanners and digital processes, so a service takes a few minutes.

2/4

A parcel with one QR code

The problem

Today you bring a paper notice, the clerk types the data into the system by hand, then takes your lična karta (ID card) and copies it. Every service at the post office starts from scratch.

The proposal

In the Pošta Srbije app every parcel has a pickup QR code. You log into the app via eUprava with two-factor authentication, so the QR code is verified in advance. The clerk scans it and instantly sees which parcel it is, where it is and how to hand it over: nothing is typed by hand. If extra confirmation is needed, a four-digit code arrives on your phone. The same QR works for payments and other state services at the post office. ID only when a customs duty or fee must be paid, and as a separate step.

3/4

Customs, declaration and payment online

The problem

Nobody knows how customs duty and PDV (VAT) are calculated, the declaration is filled in at the counter (since 23 February 2026 Pošta Srbije charges for it), and the postman at the door wants cash.

The proposal

You fill in the declaration in the app: where the parcel comes from and what is inside. A public calculator shows how duty, PDV and fees are calculated, and everything is paid online in advance. If something still has to be paid at the door, the postman's app instantly creates an NBS IPS QR code and the recipient pays on the spot from a banking app.

4/4

Customs clearance within one working day

The problem

Parcels sit at customs for over 35 days, and there is no legal deadline for clearance. Everything is manual, so customs is overloaded.

The proposal

Target: 99.9% of parcels cleared within one working day of arrival. This is affordable because the declaration and payment are already online and in advance. The system is almost fully automated, and you can appeal the automatic decision so a human reviews it. Complex cases are handled online, and if needed you can come in person with documents. Less work for customs, faster and easier for everyone.
