PregledOverview

Tri zvanične brzine

Three official speeds

Ko hitno treba dokument nema legalan način da ga dobije brže, pa traži „vezu”, poznanika ili nekome plati. Predlog je da hitnost postane javna: brzinu biraš pri podnošenju, taksa je zvanična, a novac ide u budžet službe.

Anyone who needs a document urgently has no legal way to get it faster, so they look for a connection, an acquaintance or someone to pay. The proposal makes urgency public: you pick the speed when filing, the fee is official and the money goes to the office budget.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Ovo ne pogoduje bogatima?This favours the rich?

Standardna brzina i njena taksa ostaju. Danas hitnost već postoji, samo kroz vezu koja je nezvanična i nejednaka. Predlog je čini javnom i jednakom.

The standard speed and its fee stay. Today urgency already exists, only through connections that are unofficial and unequal. The proposal makes it public and equal.

Neće li standard postati sporiji?Won't the standard get slower?

To je rizik koji treba pratiti. Zato je predlog povezan sa javnom tablom kvaliteta usluga, gde se vide stvarni rokovi.

That is a risk to watch. This is why the proposal ties in with a public dashboard of service quality, where real deadlines show.
