Zamena strane vozačke dozvole

Exchanging a foreign driving licence

Zamena strane vozačke dozvole bez nagađanja.

Exchange a foreign driving licence without the guesswork.

Novi zakon · Odluka Skupštine · 2+ godine

A new law · A decision of Parliament · 2+ years

ProblemThe problem

Pravilo postoji, uputstvo ne

The rule exists, the instructions do not

Stranac sa odobrenim stalnim boravkom, kao i državljanin Srbije koji se vraća da stalno živi u zemlji, sme da vozi sa stranom vozačkom dozvolom do šest meseci od ulaska u zemlju. Posle toga mora da je zameni ili da izvadi novu srpsku.

A foreigner with approved permanent residence, like a Serbian citizen returning to live in the country, may drive on a foreign licence for up to six months from entering Serbia. After that they must exchange it or apply for a new Serbian one.

Zvanične stranice za strance to kažu, ali ne opisuju kako se zamena radi: gde, sa kojim dokumentima i da li treba polagati. Zato se odgovori traže po blogovima i forumima.

The official pages for foreigners say so, but do not describe how an exchange is done: where, with which documents, and whether a test is needed. So answers are sought on blogs and forums.

Kako radiHow it works

Četiri dela rešenja

Four parts of the fix

  1. 1

    Pravila po zemlji

    Rules per country

    Za svaku zemlju izdavanja piše da li se dozvola priznaje, da li treba polaganje, koja su dokumenta i koliko košta.

    For each country of issue it says whether the licence is recognised, whether a test is needed, which documents are required and what it costs.

  2. 2

    Jedan onlajn tok

    One online flow

    Zahtev, dokumenta i taksa u istom koraku, bez prepisivanja podataka.

    The application, documents and fee in one step, without retyping data.

  3. 3

    Uživo samo što mora

    In person only what must be

    Termin se zakazuje samo za korak koji se ne može obaviti onlajn.

    An appointment is booked only for the step that cannot be done online.

  4. 4

    Status i rok

    Status and deadline

    Vidiš gde je zahtev i do kada stiže odluka.

    You see where the application is and when a decision is due.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Menja li ovo ko mora da zameni dozvolu?Does this change who has to exchange a licence?

Ne. Pravilo od šest meseci ostaje kakvo jeste, predlog samo čini jasnim kako se postupa.

No. The six-month rule stays as it is; the proposal only makes it clear how to proceed.

Šta je sa bezbednošću u saobraćaju?What about road safety?

Provere koje danas važe ostaju. Razlika je u tome što ih unapred vidiš i što se dokumenta ne nose više puta.

The checks that apply today stay. The difference is that you see them in advance and do not carry documents more than once.

Zašto javna stranica po zemljama?Why a public page per country?

Jer pravila treba da budu vidljiva na jednom mestu, a ne da se saznaju tek na šalteru.

Because the rules should be visible in one place, not discovered at the counter.
