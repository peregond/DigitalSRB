PregledOverview

Zatvaranje firme u četiri koraka

Closing a business in four steps

Elektronska registracija preduzetnika već postoji, a od decembra 2023. i onlajn prijava brisanja preko portala APR-a. Problem je što konačan obračun poreza često stigne tek 2–3 meseca posle odjave.

Electronic registration of a preduzetnik (sole trader) already exists, and since December 2023 so does an online deregistration request on the APR portal. The problem is that the final tax assessment often arrives only 2–3 months after deregistration.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Firma ima nepodmirene obaveze?The firm has unpaid obligations?

Konačan obračun ih pokazuje odmah, pa nema iznenađenja posle zatvaranja. Plaćanje ide u istom koraku.

The final assessment shows them at once, so there is no surprise after closing. Payment happens in the same step.

Može li država da tako obračuna sve?Can the state calculate it all that fast?

Sve što je prijavljeno već je u njenim evidencijama. Ono što fali, na primer izveštaj, rešava se doplatom razlike.

Everything reported is already in its records. What is missing, such as a report, is handled by topping up the difference.

Zašto ne ostati kod današnjeg?Why not keep today's way?

Jer neizvesnost nekoliko meseci posle zatvaranja košta ljude i vreme i nervozu, a država ionako ima podatke.

Because months of uncertainty after closing cost people time and stress, and the state has the data anyway.
