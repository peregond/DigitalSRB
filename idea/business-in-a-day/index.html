ProblemThe problem

Zatvaranje traje, a račun stiže kasnije

Closing takes time, and the bill comes later

Već postoji elektronska registracija preduzetnika, a od decembra 2023. i onlajn prijava brisanja preko portala APR-a.

Electronic registration of a preduzetnik (sole trader) already exists, and since December 2023 so does an online deregistration request on the APR portal.

Ali brisanje i dalje traje, a konačan obračun poreza često stigne 2–3 meseca posle odjave. Ljudi zatvore firmu, a ne znaju koliko duguju.

But closing still takes time, and the final tax assessment often arrives 2–3 months after deregistration. People close a business without knowing what they owe.

Zatvaranje firme za jedan dan

Closing a business in one day

  1. 1

    Podnosiš prijavu onlajn

    File the request online

    Jedan zahtev u APR-u, bez dolaska na šalter.

    One request at APR, no visit to a counter.

  2. 2

    Poreska obračunava odmah

    Tax is calculated at once

    Poreska uprava iz podataka koje već ima izračuna konačan porez dok čekaš.

    Poreska uprava works out the final tax from data it already holds, while you wait.

  3. 3

    Vidiš iznos i plaćaš

    You see the amount and pay

    Konačan porez je na ekranu pri odjavi i plaća se odmah, bez rešenja posle nekoliko meseci.

    The final tax is on screen at deregistration and is paid at once, with no decision months later.

  4. 4

    Firma je zatvorena

    The business is closed

    Brisanje i plaćanje završavaju se istog radnog dana.

    Deletion and payment finish on the same working day.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Firma ima nepodmirene obaveze?The firm has unpaid obligations?

Konačan obračun ih pokazuje odmah, pa nema iznenađenja posle zatvaranja. Plaćanje ide u istom koraku.

The final assessment shows them at once, so there is no surprise after closing. Payment happens in the same step.

Može li država da tako obračuna sve?Can the state calculate it all that fast?

Sve što je prijavljeno već je u njenim evidencijama. Ono što fali, na primer izveštaj, rešava se doplatom razlike.

Everything reported is already in its records. What is missing, such as a report, is handled by topping up the difference.

Zašto ne ostati kod današnjeg?Why not keep today's way?

Jer neizvesnost nekoliko meseci posle zatvaranja košta ljude i vreme i nervozu, a država ionako ima podatke.

Because months of uncertainty after closing cost people time and stress, and the state has the data anyway.
