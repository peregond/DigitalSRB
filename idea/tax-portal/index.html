Objediniti portale APR-a i ePoreza

Merge the APR and ePorezi portals

Registracija firme i plaćanje poreza na jednom portalu.

Register a business and pay taxes on one portal.

Odmah · Samo tehnika · 3–12 meseci

Right away · Technology only · 3–12 months

PregledOverview

Jedan portal umesto dva

One portal instead of two

Danas firmu registruješ u jednom elektronskom sistemu, a poreze plaćaš u drugom, pa moraš da prelaziš sa portala na portal. Predlog je jedinstveni poreski portal u kome je sve to na jednom mestu.

Today you register a business in one electronic system and pay taxes in another, so you have to switch from portal to portal. The proposal is a single tax portal where it is all in one place.

Kako izgledaWhat it looks like

Jedan portal, jedan pogled

One portal, one view

Danas ePorezi nudi tri načina prijave i programe za preuzimanje, a APR ima poseban portal sa svojim uslugama. Jedinstveni portal sve to prikazuje na jednom ekranu, na jednom nalogu.

Today ePorezi offers three ways to sign in and programs to download, and APR has its own portal with its own services. The single portal shows it all on one screen, on one account.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Da li se APR i Poreska uprava spajaju?Are APR and Poreska uprava merged?

Ne. Ustanove ostaju nezavisne. Spajaju se samo njihove elektronske usluge, na jednom portalu.

No. The agencies stay independent. Only their electronic services are brought together, on one portal.

Ko odgovara za podatke?Who is responsible for the data?

Svaka ustanova za svoje podatke i odluke, kao i danas. Portal ih samo prikazuje na jednom mestu.

Each agency for its own data and decisions, as today. The portal only shows them in one place.
