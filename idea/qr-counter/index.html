Jedan QR kod za svaki šalter

One QR code for every counter

Skeniraš jednom. Službenik već zna tvoj slučaj.

Scan once. The clerk already knows your case.

Odmah · Samo tehnika · 3–12 meseci

Right away · Technology only · 3–12 months

  • 1QR kod po predmetuQR code per case
  • 0zakona za izmenulaws to change
  • 3–12meseci, okvirnomonths, roughly

ProblemThe problem

Svaki put sve iz početka

Starting from zero every time

Na šalteru u MUP-u, APR-u ili opštini moraš sam da objasniš šta ti treba. Službenik pogađa koji je postupak, traži papire i ručno kuca podatke u sistem.

At a MUP, APR or opština counter you have to explain what you need yourself. The clerk guesses which procedure applies, asks for papers and types the data into the system by hand.

Svaki razgovor zavisi od iskustva i raspoloženja dvoje ljudi. Gde nema jasnih pravila i procesa, lični odnos između vlasti i građanina postaje prostor za „vezu” i za koruptivne šeme.

Every conversation depends on the experience and mood of two people. Where there are no clear rules and processes, the personal relationship between the authorities and the citizen becomes room for “connections” and corruption schemes.

DanasToday

Četiri stvari koje se stalno ponavljaju

Four things that keep repeating

Objašnjavaš iznova

You explain, again

Ista priča na svakom šalteru, iako država već zna ko si i šta si podneo.

The same story at every counter, although the state already knows who you are and what you filed.

Pogađanje postupka

Guessing the procedure

Službenik mora da pogodi koji se postupak odnosi na tebe.

The clerk has to guess which procedure applies to you.

Papiri na zahtev

Papers on request

Traže se dokumenta koja su možda već u sistemu.

Documents are requested that may already be in the system.

Ručno kucanje

Typing by hand

Podaci se prepisuju, a svako prepisivanje je prilika za grešku.

Data is retyped, and every retyping is a chance for a mistake.

PredlogThe proposal

Šta službenik vidi posle skeniranja

What the clerk sees after the scan

Umesto pitanja „šta vam treba?” službenik dobija ekran sa tvojim predmetom: šta je podneto, šta nedostaje i koji je sledeći korak.

Instead of “what do you need?” the clerk gets a screen with your case: what was filed, what is missing and what comes next.

Kako radiHow it works

Četiri koraka

Four steps

  1. 1

    Predmet dobija QR

    Every case gets a QR

    U aplikaciji eUprava svaki predmet ima lični QR kod. Nastaje kada podneseš zahtev ili zakažeš termin.

    In the eUprava app every case has a personal QR code. It appears when you file a request or book an appointment.

  2. 2

    Pokažeš ga na šalteru

    Show it at the counter

    Telefonom, a ko nema pametan telefon, odštampanim kodom ili linkom iz SMS-a.

    On your phone or, for people without a smartphone, a printed code or an SMS link.

  3. 3

    Službenik skenira

    The clerk scans

    U sistemu se odmah otvara tvoj konkretan slučaj, sa istorijom i statusom.

    Your specific case opens in the system at once, with its history and status.

  4. 4

    Sistem vodi dalje

    The system takes it forward

    Službenik postavlja samo pitanja koja stvarno nedostaju, a ti u aplikaciji vidiš novi status.

    The clerk asks only what is really missing, and you see the new status in the app.

Za kogaWho benefits

Dobiti mogu svi

Everyone gains

Građani

Citizens

Ništa ne objašnjavaš, manje papira i manje odlazaka.

Nothing to explain, fewer papers and fewer trips.

Stranci

Foreigners

Isti tok na engleskom, bez potrebe da znaš lokalni žargon.

The same flow in English, without needing to know the local jargon.

Službenici

Clerks

Jedan standardan ekran, manje kucanja i manje sporova.

One standard screen, less typing and fewer disputes.

Država

The state

Manje grešaka, jasan trag svakog koraka i manje prostora za „vezu”.

Fewer errors, a clear trace of every step and less room for “connections”.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Nemam pametan telefon?I have no smartphone?

Kod može da se odštampa ili stigne kao link u SMS-u. Šalter ostaje otvoren i za one koji dođu bez ičega, samo sa dužim postupkom.

The code can be printed or arrive as an SMS link. The counter stays open for people who arrive with nothing, just with a longer procedure.

Je li bezbedno pokazati QR kod?Is it safe to show a QR code?

QR sadrži samo nasumičnu oznaku predmeta. Službenik mora da bude prijavljen, a svaki pristup tvom predmetu beleži se i vidljiv ti je u aplikaciji.

The QR holds only a random case reference. The clerk must be logged in, and every access to your case is logged and visible to you in the app.

Sistem ne radi?The system is down?

Postupak se vraća na današnji, papirni. QR ne uvodi novu zavisnost, samo skraćuje put.

The procedure falls back to today's paper one. The QR adds no new dependency, it only shortens the path.
