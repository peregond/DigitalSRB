PregledOverview

Četiri dela jedne reforme

Four parts of one reform

Pošta Srbije je mesto gde se država susreće sa građaninom svakodnevno, a veliki deo posla još ide na papiru. Predlog ima četiri dela, od aplikacije do carine.

Pošta Srbije is where the state meets the citizen every day, and much of the work still runs on paper. The proposal has four parts, from the app to customs.

1/41/4

Sve poštanske usluge u jednoj aplikaciji

All postal services in one app

Mnogo toga na pošti još se radi na papiru i ručno, a red u poslovnici traje. Poslovnice dobijaju skenere i digitalne procese, pa se usluga obavi za nekoliko minuta.

Much at the pošta is still done on paper and by hand, and the queue at the branch drags on. Branches get scanners and digital processes, so a service takes a few minutes.

2/42/4

Paket jednim QR kodom

A parcel with one QR code

QR je unapred potvrđen jer je prijava u aplikaciju već dvofaktorska. Isti QR radi za plaćanja i druge državne usluge na pošti.

The QR is verified in advance because the app login is already two-factor. The same QR works for payments and other state services at the post office.

3/43/4

Carina, deklaracija i plaćanje onlajn

Customs, declaration and payment online

Danas se ne zna kako se računaju carina i PDV, deklaracija se popunjava na šalteru (od 23. februara 2026. Pošta je i naplaćuje), a poštar na adresi traži gotovinu.

Today nobody knows how duty and PDV are calculated, the declaration is filled in at the counter (since 23 February 2026 Pošta charges for it), and the postman at the door wants cash.

  1. 1

    Deklaracija u aplikaciji

    Declaration in the app

    Navodiš odakle stiže pošiljka i šta je u njoj.

    You say where the parcel comes from and what is inside.

  2. 2

    Javni kalkulator

    A public calculator

    Pokazuje kako se računaju carina, PDV i naknade, pa se zna unapred koliko košta.

    Shows how duty, PDV and fees are calculated, so the cost is known in advance.

  3. 3

    Plaćanje unapred

    Pay in advance

    Sve se plaća onlajn pre dolaska paketa.

    Everything is paid online before the parcel arrives.

  4. 4

    Doplata na vratima

    Top-up at the door

    Ako ipak treba doplatiti, poštar u aplikaciji odmah pravi NBS IPS QR, a ti plaćaš iz bankarske aplikacije.

    If something is still due, the postman's app instantly creates an NBS IPS QR and you pay from your banking app.

4/44/4

Carinjenje za jedan radni dan

Customs clearance within one working day

Cilj je 99,9% pošiljki ocarinjeno u roku od jednog radnog dana. To je izvodljivo jer su deklaracija i plaćanje već onlajn i unapred, a sistem je gotovo potpuno automatizovan.

The target is 99.9% of parcels cleared within one working day of arrival. That is feasible because the declaration and payment are already online and in advance, and the system is almost fully automated.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Nemam aplikaciju?I do not have the app?

Lična karta i šalter ostaju za one koji dođu, samo sa sporijim tokom. Aplikacija je prečica, ne obaveza.

The ID and the counter stay for people who come in, just with a slower flow. The app is a shortcut, not a duty.

Šta ako automat pogreši?What if the machine is wrong?

Žališ se i tada odluku pregleda čovek. Složeni slučajevi idu onlajn, a po potrebi možeš doći sa dokumentima.

You appeal and a human reviews the decision. Complex cases go online, and if needed you can come in with documents.
