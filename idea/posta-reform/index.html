Reforma pošte

Pošta reform

Digitalizujemo sve što se i dalje radi na papiru i ukidamo ručni posao koji usporava svaki korak pošte: od slanja i obrade do isporuke i preuzimanja.

We digitalise what is still done on paper and remove the manual work that slows every stage of the post: from sending and processing to delivery and pickup.

Odmah · Samo tehnika · 3–12 meseci

Right away · Technology only · 3–12 months

Four steps of one reform

Pošta Srbije je mesto gde se država svakodnevno susreće sa građaninom, a veliki deo posla još ide na papiru. Predlog ima četiri koraka.

Pošta Srbije is where the state meets the citizen every day, and much of the work still runs on paper. The proposal has four steps.

1/41/4

Analiza svega što je još na papiru

An audit of everything still on paper

Pre nego što gradimo nešto novo, radimo punu analizu svega što pošta danas radi na papiru i ručno, i pravimo jasan proces kako da se sve to digitalizuje. Cilj je da 99,9% procesa bude elektronsko.

Before building anything new, we run a full analysis of everything the post still does on paper and by hand, and design a clear process for digitising all of it. The goal is for 99.9% of processes to be digital.

    Popis

    Inventory

    Svaki papir, obrazac, pečat i šalterski korak u pošti, na jednom spisku.

    Every paper, form, stamp and counter step in the post, on one list.

  2. 2

    Mapa procesa

    Process map

    Za svaki korak: ko ga radi, koji se podaci unose i kuda idu dalje.

    For each step: who does it, which data is entered and where it goes next.

  3. 3

    Novi tok

    New flow

    Svaki papirni korak dobija jasan digitalni, bez duplih unosa i kopija.

    Each paper step gets a clear digital one, with no double entry or copies.

  4. 4

    Digitalizacija

    Digitise

    Digitalizuje se sve što može, do cilja od 99,9% elektronskih procesa.

    Everything that can be digital goes digital, up to the target of 99.9% electronic processes.

2/42/4

Nova aplikacija za klijente

A new app for customers

Jedna aplikacija za sve poštanske usluge: prijava preko eUprave, praćenje, preuzimanje paketa QR kodom, carina i plaćanje. U istom dizajnu kao druge državne usluge, bez papirnih obaveštenja i formulara.

One app for every postal service: sign-in through eUprava, tracking, pickup with a QR code, customs and payment. In the same design as other state services, with no paper notices or forms.

AI

Vizualizacija je napravljena uz pomoć veštačke inteligencije (AI) i predstavlja koncept. Imena, brojevi pošiljki, iznosi i poslovnice su primeri.

This visual was created with the help of AI and shows a concept. Names, tracking numbers, amounts and branches are examples.

Paket jednim QR kodomA parcel with one QR code

QR je unapred potvrđen jer je prijava u aplikaciju već dvofaktorska. Isti QR radi za plaćanja i druge državne usluge na pošti.

The QR is verified in advance because the app login is already two-factor. The same QR works for payments and other state services at the post office.

3/43/4

Nova aplikacija za zaposlene

A new app for staff

Isti sistem iznutra: šalter, upravnik poslovnice, logistika, carina i poštar rade u jednom okruženju. Službenik skenira QR i vidi ceo slučaj, a podaci se ne kucaju ponovo. Poslovnice dobijaju skenere i digitalne procese, pa se usluga obavi za nekoliko minuta.

The same system from the inside: the counter, the branch manager, logistics, customs and the postman all work in one environment. The clerk scans a QR and sees the whole case, with no retyping of data. Branches get scanners and digital processes, so a service takes a few minutes.

4/44/4

Sve carinske procedure onlajn i ubrzano carinjenje

Every customs procedure online, and faster clearance

Danas se deklaracija popunjava na šalteru (od 23. februara 2026. Pošta je i naplaćuje), poštar na adresi traži gotovinu, a paketi stoje na carini i više od 35 dana, bez zakonskog roka. Cilj je da sve carinske procedure budu onlajn i da 99,9% pošiljki bude ocarinjeno u roku od jednog radnog dana.

Today the declaration is filled in at the counter (since 23 February 2026 Pošta Srbije charges for it), the postman at the door wants cash, and parcels sit at customs for over 35 days with no legal deadline. The goal is for every customs procedure to be online and for 99.9% of parcels to be cleared within one working day.

Deklaracija i plaćanje onlajnDeclaration and payment online

Deklarišeš u aplikaciji, unapred vidiš javni obračun i plaćaš onlajn, a ako ipak treba doplatiti na adresi, koristiš NBS IPS QR.

You declare in the app, see a public calculation up front and pay online, and if something is still due at the door you use an NBS IPS QR.

Carinjenje za jedan radni danClearance within one working day

Sistem je gotovo potpuno automatizovan, a na odluku automata možeš da se žališ, pa je pregleda živ čovek.

The system is almost fully automated, and you can appeal an automatic decision so a human reviews it.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Nemam aplikaciju?I do not have the app?

Lična karta i šalter ostaju za one koji dođu, samo sa sporijim tokom. Aplikacija je prečica, ne obaveza.

The ID and the counter stay for people who come in, just with a slower flow. The app is a shortcut, not a duty.

Šta ako automat pogreši?What if the machine is wrong?

Žališ se i tada odluku pregleda čovek. Složeni slučajevi idu onlajn, a po potrebi možeš doći sa dokumentima.

You appeal and a human reviews the decision. Complex cases go online, and if needed you can come in with documents.
