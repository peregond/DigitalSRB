Lična karta i boravak u Apple i Google Wallet-u

ID and residence card in Apple and Google Wallet

Lična karta i boravak u novčaniku na telefonu.

Your ID and residence card in your phone wallet.

Novi zakon · Odluka Skupštine · 2+ godine

A new law · A decision of Parliament · 2+ years

  • 18013-5ISO standard za mobilne dokumenteISO standard for mobile documents
  • EUDIevropski novčanik digitalnog identitetathe EU digital identity wallet
  • 1 sprovera, QR ili NFCto check, QR or NFC

ProblemThe problem

Kartica se gubi i uvek mora da se nosi

A card gets lost and must always be carried

Fizička lična karta i boravišna dozvola gube se, zaboravljaju kod kuće i moraju da se pokažu pri svakoj proveri, a pokazuješ sve što je na njima.

A physical ID and residence card gets lost, is forgotten at home and has to be shown at every check, and you show everything printed on it.

PredlogThe proposal

Isti dokument, u telefonu

The same document, in your phone

Digitalna lična karta i boravišna dozvola u Apple i Google novčaniku, po standardu ISO 18013-5 i evropskom EUDI novčaniku, sa istom pravnom snagom kao kartica.

A digital ID and residence card in Apple and Google Wallet, following ISO 18013-5 and the EU EUDI wallet, with the same legal force as the card.

Pokazuješ samo potrebnoShare only what is needed

Provera koja ne otkriva sve

A check that does not reveal everything

Čitač traži samo jedan podatak, na primer da si punoletan. Ti potvrđuješ, a JMBG i adresa ostaju u telefonu.

The reader asks for just one fact, for example that you are over 18. You approve, and the personal number and address stay in your phone.

Šta treba da postojiWhat has to be in place

Četiri uslova

Four conditions

Težina je visoka: potreban je zakon, a ne samo tehnika.

Difficulty is high: it takes a law, not just technology.

  1. 1

    Zakon

    A law

    Digitalna isprava dobija istu pravnu snagu kao kartica.

    The digital document gets the same legal force as the card.

  2. 2

    ISO 18013-5

    ISO 18013-5

    Usklađenost sa standardom za mobilne vozačke i lične isprave.

    Compliance with the standard for mobile driving licences and IDs.

  3. 3

    Apple i Google

    Apple and Google

    Dogovor sa kompanijama koje drže novčanike.

    An agreement with the companies that run the wallets.

  4. 4

    EUDI

    EUDI

    Praćenje istog evropskog standarda koji EU uvodi, da isprava radi i van Srbije.

    Following the same European standard the EU is introducing, so the document works beyond Serbia.

Prednosti

Benefits

Građani

Citizens

Dokument je uvek u telefonu i ne može da se izgubi kao kartica.

The document is always on your phone and cannot be lost like a card.

Stranci

Foreigners

Boravišna dozvola uvek uz sebe, na novčaniku koji već koriste.

The residence permit always at hand, in the wallet they already use.

Provera

Checks

Takes a second and shows only what is necessary.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Telefon ostane bez baterije ili se izgubi?My phone dies or is lost?

Pitanje za zakon i uslove izdavanja. Predlog daje digitalnoj ispravi istu snagu kao kartici, a da li plastična kartica ostaje uz nju odlučuje zakon.

A question for the law and the issuing rules. The proposal gives the digital document the same force as the card, and whether the plastic card stays alongside is for the law to decide.

Zašto baš ISO 18013-5 i EUDI?Why exactly ISO 18013-5 and EUDI?

Zato što su to standardi koje Apple, Google i EU već koriste, pa isprava radi bez posebnih rešenja.

Because these are the standards Apple, Google and the EU already use, so the document works without special arrangements.
