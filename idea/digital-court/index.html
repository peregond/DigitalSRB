A digital court

Digitalni sud za više od milion nerešenih predmeta.

A digital court for over a million pending cases.

ProblemThe problem

Više od milion predmeta čeka

Over a million cases are waiting

Na kraju 2024. nerešeno je bilo 1.042.252 predmeta, od toga 221.305 starijih od 3 godine i 9.498 starijih od 10 godina.

At the end of 2024 there were 1,042,252 pending cases, 221,305 older than 3 years and 9,498 older than 10 years.

Svaki od tih predmeta je čovek ili firma koji čekaju odluku. Što duže traje, to je više prostora za pritisak i za „vezu”.

Every one of those cases is a person or a company waiting for a decision. The longer it takes, the more room there is for pressure and for “connections”.

Zašto automatski rasporedWhy automatic assignment

Sudija po pravilu, ne po vezi

A judge by rule, not by connection

Kada se sudija bira ručno, postoji prostor da se bira „pravi” sudija. Automatski raspored uklanja taj izbor, a javan status smanjuje prostor za dugo čekanje bez objašnjenja.

When a judge is picked by hand, there is room to pick the “right” judge. Automatic assignment removes that choice, and a public status leaves less room for long waits without explanation.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Svako ročište ide onlajn?Every hearing goes online?

Predlog obuhvata ročišta onlajn. Za koje je predmete potrebno prisustvo odlučuje procesni zakon.

The proposal covers online hearings. Which cases need presence is for the procedural law to decide.

Ko vidi status predmeta?Who sees a case's status?

Javni status pokazuje gde predmet stoji. Šta tačno se vidi određuje zakon, uz zaštitu ličnih podataka.

A public status shows where the case stands. Exactly what is visible is for the law to define, with personal data protected.
