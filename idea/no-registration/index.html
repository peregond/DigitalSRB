Ukinuti prijavu boravka stranaca

Abolish registration of stay for foreigners

Nema više prijave boravka stranaca u roku od 24 sata.

No more 24-hour registration of stay for foreigners.

ProblemThe problem

Beli karton: obaveza koja ništa ne dodaje

The beli karton: a duty that adds nothing

Stranac mora da prijavi adresu u roku od 24 časa od dolaska i posle svake promene, a stanodavci i hoteli moraju da ga prijave policiji.

A foreigner must register their address within 24 hours of arrival and after every move, and landlords and hotels must report them to the police.

To je stres za ljude, posao za stanodavce i gomila papira za policiju, a ne donosi bezbednost koju granica već ne obezbeđuje.

It means stress for people, work for landlords and piles of paper for the police, without adding security the border does not already provide.

Šta je dovoljnoWhat is enough

Granična baza već sve zna

The border database already knows

Granična policija ima bazu ulazaka i izlazaka. Iz nje se zna ko je ušao, kada i koliko dana sme da ostane, bez vize ili po vizi.

The border police already hold an entry and exit database. It tells who entered, when, and how many days they may stay visa-free or on a visa.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Država neće znati gde stranac živi?The state will not know where a foreigner lives?

Argument predloga je da adresna prijava ne dodaje bezbednost koju granica već ne daje: zna se ko je ušao, kada i do kada sme da ostane.

The proposal's argument is that address registration adds no security the border does not already give: it is known who entered, when and until when they may stay.

A boravišne dozvole?What about residence permits?

To su zasebni postupci i zasebne ideje, na primer jasni rokovi za boravišne dozvole.

Those are separate procedures and separate ideas, for example clear deadlines for residence permits.
