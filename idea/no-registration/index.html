PregledOverview

Zašto prijava boravka ništa ne dodaje

Why registering a stay adds nothing

Stranac mora da prijavi adresu u roku od 24 časa od dolaska i posle svake promene, a stanodavci i hoteli moraju da ga prijave policiji. To je stres za ljude, posao za stanodavce i gomila papira, bez bezbednosti koju granica već ne obezbeđuje.

A foreigner must register their address within 24 hours of arrival and after every move, and landlords and hotels must report them to the police. It means stress for people, work for landlords and piles of paper, without adding security the border does not already provide.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Država neće znati gde stranac živi?The state will not know where a foreigner lives?

Argument predloga je da adresna prijava ne dodaje bezbednost koju granica već ne daje: zna se ko je ušao, kada i do kada sme da ostane.

The proposal's argument is that address registration adds no security the border does not already give: it is known who entered, when and until when they may stay.

A boravišne dozvole?What about residence permits?

To su zasebni postupci i zasebne ideje, na primer jasni rokovi za boravišne dozvole.

Those are separate procedures and separate ideas, for example clear deadlines for residence permits.
