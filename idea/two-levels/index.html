PregledOverview

Dva nivoa umesto tri

Two levels instead of three

Već postoji: eID ima tri načina prijave, lozinku, aplikaciju ConsentID i kvalifikovani sertifikat (čip lična karta ili oblak sertifikat). Za viši nivo aktivacioni kod se podiže na šalteru ili preko sertifikata, a tri nivoa zbunjuju ljude.

Already exists: eID has three ways to log in, a password, the ConsentID app and a qualified certificate (chip ID card or cloud certificate). For the higher level the activation code is collected at a counter or via a certificate, and three levels confuse people.

Pitanja i odgovoriQuestions and answers

Šta ako…

What if…

Nemam račun u banci?I do not have a bank account?

Identitet možeš da potvrdiš i čip ličnom kartom ili preko pošte. Banka je samo jedna od tri mogućnosti.

You can also confirm your identity with a chip ID card or through the pošta. The bank is just one of three options.

Šta je sa strancima?What about foreigners?

Dobijaju osnovni pristup odmah, a puni čim dobiju boravak.

They get basic access at once and full access once they have residence.
